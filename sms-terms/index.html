Program

ImReadyToScan sends prep video links for scheduled imaging exams.

ImReadyToScan, LLC is the sender of all messages and is responsible for this SMS program and your data.

How you sign up

When you book your exam by phone, your facility's scheduler asks:

"Would you like ImReadyToScan to text you a short video on how to prepare for your exam? You'll get up to 2 texts per scheduled exam. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent is not required to receive care. Is it OK to text you at this mobile number?"

Frequency

Up to 2 messages per scheduled exam.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt out and get help

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email dennis@imreadytoscan.com.

Consent and care

Consent is not a condition of care.
