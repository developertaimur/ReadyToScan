Scheduler time

Less prep talk on every scheduling call.

The video gives the prep instructions, so schedulers do not have to read long prep rules on the phone. That saves time on every call, so the same team can book more patients. The pilot will measure the effect at your site. We pick the right video from the exam description. If a description is unclear, a person checks it. We never guess.

Simple for your team

A green check for every patient who watched.

We handle the texts and videos. Once a week, you upload your show-up report from Epic, and that's it. You only pay for patients who confirmed and showed up.

At the end of each video, the patient taps one button. A green check then shows next to that patient on your team's dashboard. A green check means the patient watched the video to the end. It does not mean the prep is done, and it does not mean the patient is cleared to arrive.

Made for your facility.

Every video and text can carry your own logo, colors and name. We can also tailor the prep steps to match how your site does each exam. Your patients see your brand, not ours. Custom branding has a small added fee.

Capacity protection

A short call list for your staff.

If a text fails, or a patient has not watched within 24 hours, that patient goes on a short call list. If you choose, your schedulers can call just those patients and fill open slots early. It's optional. ImReadyToScan does not book, cancel, or fill appointments. Your staff stays in charge of the schedule.

Patient preparation that protects imaging capacity, scheduler time, and the care experience.
