3. What we do

We help hospitals and imaging centers send patients a short video on how to prepare for an imaging exam. We are not a health care provider. We do not book, cancel, or change appointments.

4. SMS Program: ImReadyToScan Prep Video Texts

Program name

ImReadyToScan Prep Video Texts.

What the texts are

These are transactional texts. We send them about an exam you scheduled with your hospital or imaging center (your "facility"). Each text has a link to a short video on how to prepare for your scheduled exam. At the end of the video, you can tap a button to confirm you watched it.

No marketing

We never send marketing, promotional, or sales texts in this program.

How you agree to get texts

You agree by voice when you schedule your exam. The scheduler asks:

"Would you like ImReadyToScan to text you a short video on how to prepare for your exam? You'll get up to 2 texts per scheduled exam. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent is not required to receive care. Is it OK to text you at this mobile number?"

Your answer is noted on the facility's schedule file. If no answer is noted, we do not text you. Agreeing to texts is not required to get care.

How many texts

Up to 2 messages per scheduled exam. The first is usually sent when the exam is booked. The second is a reminder before the exam. If you were missed the first time, you may get only the reminder. We do not send the same text twice for the same exam. We will also reply if you text STOP or HELP.

Cost

Msg & data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's normal rates apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP at any time. You can also reply STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. We will send one message to confirm. Then you will get no more texts from this program. Your facility may still call you about your exam.

How to get help

Reply HELP. Or email dennis@imreadytoscan.com. For questions about your exam, call your facility.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your mobile carrier and is not fully in our control.

Not medical advice

The videos give general preparation information. They are not medical advice.

  • Always follow your facility's instructions. If the video and your facility's instructions are different, follow your facility.
  • If you have questions about your exam, your medicines, or your health, call your care team.
  • In an emergency, call 911.

Tapping the button means you watched the video. It does not mean your preparation is complete. It does not mean you are cleared for your exam.
