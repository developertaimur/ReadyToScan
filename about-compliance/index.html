Our founding philosophy

Built with imaging professionals.

ImReadyToScan, LLC was founded by Dennis E. Love in Brevard County, Florida. Our prep scripts were reviewed by a nuclear medicine technologist. Before any text goes out, a technologist at each site confirms that site's own rules. We use real people on video, in plain words, so patients know what to expect.

Fair evaluation

Independent evaluation.

Anyone at ImReadyToScan with a family member at a hospital we work with stays out of that hospital's review and buying decisions. The hospital's own team decides.

What we store.

We do not connect to your systems. The file you upload may include first name, last name, mobile number, exam description, and appointment date and time. We store it only to send the right video and record the click. Once a week, your team uploads a show-up report from Epic. It only shows which patients came in. We use it for billing, and it doesn't connect to Epic. A business associate agreement (BAA) is signed before the first upload. Patient information is never sold or used for marketing.
